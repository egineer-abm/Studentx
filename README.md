## 🚀 How to Utilize This Ecosystem
To get the most out of this project, understand the distinction between our **Interactive Skill** (Agentic AI) and our **Portable Knowledge Base** (Markdown).

### 1. The Interactive Experience
If you use **Gemini CLI and Other AI Agents like Cusror, Claude code or Codex, etc.**, you have access to the **CS-Mentor** an advanced, self-improving AI tutor. It does not just provide information; it *guides* your learning process through interactive conversation.

*   **Install:** `skills install cs-mentor.skill --scope user [repo link]`
*   **Activate:** `/skills reload`
*   **Use Strategically:**
    *   `/mentor-path`: Start here to build your personalized roadmap.
    *   `/deep-dive <topic>`: Use when you need a complex concept broken down.
    *   `/project-audit`: Use this to get professional architectural feedback on your code.
    *   *The Mentor learns from your feedback—provide it often to improve future guidance.*

## 💡 The "Curation over Accumulation" Philosophy
**Stop drowning in information.**
We follow the **70/30 Rule**: Spend **70% of your time building/debugging** and **30% consuming theory**. Use the **CS-Mentor** or any AI agent to help you *apply* what you read, not just to read more.
