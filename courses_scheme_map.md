### Core Engineering Courses (97 credits)
ICT, Computer Engineering Workshop, **Calculus and Analytical Geometry**, Applied Physics, Computer Programming, Linear Circuit Analysis, Electronic Devices & Circuits, Linear Algebra, OOP, CAD, Digital Logic Design, Discrete Structures, Differential Equations, **Numerical Methods**, Data Structures & Algorithms, Computer Architecture, Signals & Systems, Complex Variables, Computer Networks, Operating Systems, Microprocessor & Interfacing, DSP, AI/ML, Database Systems, Software Engineering, Instrumentation & Control, Probability & Statistics, Mobile App Development, Digital Systems Design, Cloud Computing, Web Engineering, Deep Learning, Robotics

### General Education (6 credits)
Communication & Presentation Skills, Technical Writing, Engineering Project Management, Engineering Economics, Engineering Entrepreneurship

### Elective (3 credits)
Cloud and Distributed Computing (or other elective from electives.md) — YouTube Channels available for all elective options in [electives.md](electives.md)

### Capstone (6 credits)
Final Year Project

### Lab Courses (3 credits)
Computer Engineering Workshop, Computer Aided Design
