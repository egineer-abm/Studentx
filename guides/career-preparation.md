# Career Preparation Guide

## Resume Building

### Structure
1. **Contact Information:** Name, phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio
2. **Education:** University, degree, GPA, expected graduation, relevant coursework
3. **Technical Skills:** Programming languages, frameworks, tools, technologies
4. **Experience:** Internships, part-time jobs, research positions (reverse chronological)
5. **Projects:** 2-4 significant projects with descriptions and technologies used
6. **Achievements:** Awards, scholarships, hackathons, competitions
7. **Leadership:** Student organizations, volunteer work, mentoring

### Tips
- Keep it to one page (for students/new graduates)
- Use action verbs: developed, implemented, designed, optimized, deployed
- Quantify achievements: "Improved performance by 40%"
- Tailor your resume for each application
- Get feedback from career services, mentors, and peers

## LinkedIn Profile Optimization
- Professional headshot and banner
- Compelling headline (not just "Student")
- Detailed experience section with descriptions
- Skills section with endorsements
- Recommendations from professors, managers, colleagues
- Share projects, articles, and achievements
- Network with professionals in your field

## Interview Preparation

### Technical Interviews
- **Data Structures & Algorithms:** Arrays, strings, linked lists, trees, graphs, hash maps, stacks, queues
- **Problem Solving:** LeetCode (Easy: 50+, Medium: 100+, Hard: 20+), HackerRank, Codeforces
- **System Design:** Scalability, load balancing, caching, databases, microservices
- **Object-Oriented Design:** Design patterns, SOLID principles, UML
- **Language-Specific:** C++ (STL, pointers, memory management), Java (collections, JVM), Python (libraries, idioms)

### Behavioral Interviews
- **STAR Method:** Situation, Task, Action, Result
- Common questions: "Tell me about yourself", "Why our company?", "Strengths/weaknesses", "Conflict resolution", "Failure experience"
- Prepare 5-6 stories covering leadership, teamwork, problem-solving, failure, conflict, achievement

### Mock Interviews
- Practice with friends, career services, online platforms (Pramp, Interviewing.io)
- Record yourself to identify areas for improvement
- Time yourself for coding challenges

## Internship & Job Search

### Timeline
- **Freshman/Sophomore:** Explore interests, join clubs, attend career fairs, apply for early insight programs
- **Junior:** Apply for summer internships (August-February), attend career fairs, prepare for interviews
- **Senior:** Apply for full-time positions (September-December), attend campus recruiting events

### Platforms
- LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor
- Handshake (university career portal)
- Company career pages
- GitHub Jobs, Stack Overflow Jobs
- AngelList (startups)

### Networking
- Attend career fairs, tech talks, meetups
- Connect with alumni in your target companies
- Reach out for informational interviews
- Be active on LinkedIn and Twitter/X

## Graduate School Preparation

### If considering MS/PhD:
- Maintain high GPA (3.5+)
- Research experience (undergraduate research, REU programs)
- Strong letters of recommendation
- Prepare for GRE (if required)
- Write compelling statement of purpose
- Identify potential advisors and reach out early

## Professional Development
- **Certifications:** AWS, Azure, GCP, CompTIA, Cisco CCNA, Oracle Java
- **Open Source:** Contribute to projects on GitHub
- **Hackathons:** Participate in local and online hackathons
- **Conferences:** Attend technical conferences (local or virtual)
- **Blogging:** Write technical articles on Medium, Dev.to, or personal blog
- **Teaching:** Tutor, TA, or create tutorials
