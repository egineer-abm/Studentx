# Study Techniques Guide

## Active Learning Strategies
- **Active Recall:** Test yourself regularly without looking at notes
- **Spaced Repetition:** Review material at increasing intervals (Anki, Quizlet)
- **Feynman Technique:** Explain concepts in simple terms as if teaching a child
- **Pomodoro Technique:** 25 minutes focused study, 5 minutes break
- **Interleaving:** Mix different topics in one study session

## Note-Taking Methods
- **Cornell Method:** Divide page into notes, cues, and summary
- **Mind Mapping:** Visual diagram connecting related concepts
- **Outline Method:** Hierarchical structure of main points and subpoints
- **Digital Tools:** Notion, Obsidian, OneNote, Evernote

## Problem-Solving Approach
1. Understand the problem (read carefully, identify knowns/unknowns)
2. Devise a plan (break down, identify formulas/algorithms)
3. Execute the plan (work step by step)
4. Review and verify (check answer, alternative solutions)

## For Programming Courses
- Code daily, even if just 30 minutes
- Debug systematically: isolate, reproduce, hypothesize, test
- Read and understand error messages completely
- Write pseudocode before actual code
- Use version control (Git) for all projects

## For Math Courses
- Practice problems daily — math is a skill, not a spectator sport
- Understand the "why" behind formulas, not just memorization
- Create a formula sheet and derive key formulas from first principles
- Work through proofs step by step

## For Theory Courses
- Create concept maps showing relationships between ideas
- Write summaries after each lecture
- Form study groups for discussion and peer teaching
- Use flashcards for key terms and definitions

## Time Management
- Plan weekly schedules with dedicated study blocks
- Break large tasks into smaller, manageable chunks
- Use the Eisenhower Matrix: urgent vs. important
- Avoid multitasking — focus on one task at a time
- Review and reflect on your progress weekly

## Exam Preparation Timeline
- **4 weeks before:** Review all material, identify weak areas
- **3 weeks before:** Deep study of weak areas, practice problems
- **2 weeks before:** Full practice exams under timed conditions
- **1 week before:** Review mistakes, focus on key concepts
- **Day before:** Light review, get good sleep
- **Exam day:** Arrive early, read questions carefully, manage time
